# M5StickC Plus SE — sources

What `measurements.json` and `svg/front.svg` were measured on. The vendor files themselves are not redistributed here: product photos and drawings stay the property of M5Stack and are cited here by address only (`NOTICE.md`).

| Source | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| [Product page](https://docs.m5stack.com/en/core/StickC-Plus_SE) | SKU, SoC, peripherals, pin map |
| [Shop listing](https://shop.m5stack.com/products/m5stickc-plus-se-mini-iot-dev-kit-esp32-pico) | Feature list, battery, enclosure, "no IMU" comparison against the Plus 1.1 |
| Dimension drawing `K016-P-SE` (`model-size_page_01`, 7016 × 4961 px) | Case outline, window opening, active rectangle, BtnA slot, "M5", both side keys. Registered at 23.624 px/mm off its own `24` and `48` extension lines. |
| Straight-on front photo (shop image of the vendor BLE demo) | Everything seen through the translucent shell, and every color: registered onto the 24 × 48 mm case at 20 px/mm and measured as area means. |

The chassis is shared with the M5StickC Plus 1.1, whose own M5Stack drawing (`m5stickc-plus1`) gives the same outline, window, slot and glyph within 0.02 mm; both were re-measured when the Plus screen origin was set.
