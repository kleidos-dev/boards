# P5: the internal I2C bus answers at 0x34 and 0x51 only (2026-11-17)

Probe P5 of `../improvements.md` (items T21, T22 and the absent IMU). The case was not opened; the board was on USB with its battery fitted.

Firmware: Kleidos `m5stickc_plus_se_debug`, the image found on the board (ELF SHA-256 prefix `bed9d07ebe90eefd`, built from the Kleidos firmware of 2026-11-17).

## Command

`I2CSCAN`: the console probes every 7-bit address on the board's internal bus (SDA GPIO21, SCL GPIO22) on both I2C controllers, and on the pins swapped as a control.

## Result

| Bus | Pins | Addresses that answered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| port 1 | SDA 21, SCL 22 | `0x34`, `0x51` |
| port 0 | SDA 21, SCL 22 | `0x34`, `0x51` |
| port 1, swapped | SDA 22, SCL 21 | none |
| port 0, swapped | SDA 22, SCL 21 | none |

Both lines idled high with their pull-ups (`idle_sda=1 idle_scl=1`). The boot log of the same image names the two answers: `Axp192: battery telemetry ready at 0x34` and `Bm8563: ready at 0x51`, and `HalBootstrap: IMU init failed`.

## What it proves

The AXP192 (U2) answers at 0x34 and the BM8563 (U4) at 0x51, the only two devices on the bus, as the 2026-09-04 scan in `board.json` found. Nothing answers at 0x68 or 0x69, so no MPU-6886 (U7) is fitted on this unit, as the print's "not fitted" note says.
