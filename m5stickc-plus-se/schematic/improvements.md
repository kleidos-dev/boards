# M5Stack StickC-Plus SE: schematic improvements (triage)

Candidate additions and corrections to this board's schematic under the owner's rule of 2026-11-17 (`AGENTS.md`, "Schematics"): a detail goes in when a primary source makes it certain, a print error is corrected when the evidence settles it, and what stays uncertain is listed open. The settled additions and corrections are applied (2026-11-17) as `kleidos` entries K1-K28 in `netlist.yaml`, listed in "Applied" below and in `README.md`; the open items are listed in `README.md`, "Open questions". One row per item: its id, the claim, the evidence, and its status.

Statuses: **settled: addition** or **settled: correction** (ready to become a `kleidos` entry, with the element it would name), **settled: no change** (the print is right, or the fact is not a drawing fact), **needs bench** (a non-invasive probe below settles it), **needs datasheet** (a primary document still to read), **cannot be settled without opening the board** (stays out, listed open), **firmware** (a statement in the Kleidos repository the print contradicts; fixed there, not here).

Sources cited by short name: *print*, M5Stack's K016-P-SE schematic as `netlist.yaml` cites it; *docs page*, M5Stack's StickC-Plus SE page as `board.json` cites it (`m5-docs`); *back label*, the vendor's sticker on the SE's back as M5Stack's documentation photo shows it (drawn in `../svg/back.svg` by `_shared/back_plus_se.py`); *PICO datasheet*, Espressif's ESP32-PICO Series Datasheet v1.5; *CH552 datasheet*, WCH's CH552 datasheet (CH552DS1), read at LCSC's English translation (C111367) hosted at <https://cdn.hackaday.io/files/1696717259204064/CH552%20Datasheet_C111367.zh-CN.en.pdf> because WCH's host timed out on 2026-11-17, then re-read at WCH's original V1G (the Wayback Machine's capture of 2024-04-04), which `netlist.yaml` cites; *AXP192 summary*, the four-page AXP192 datasheet M5Stack hosts (`axp192-datasheet` in `board.json`); *M5Unified*, M5Stack's driver library at commit 96576a1; *Kleidos*, the firmware repository (paths below are in that repository); *bench scan*, `board.json`'s `bench-unit` (2026-09-04).

## Bench probes

All non-invasive: the case stays closed, and every contact is the Hat-Bus header, the Grove port or USB. Run on the Kleidos `m5stickc_plus_se_debug` image unless a probe says otherwise; record each in a bench note (date, image, readings) for the entry's citation.

| Probe | Settles | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | T01: which rail feeds VDD3P3_RTC | Needs a debug-only GPIO drive (a probe image, or a console command the debug build does not have). Drive GPIO26, an RTC IO, high; with a multimeter read the header's G26 cell (through R105 22R, no load) and its 3V3 cell against GND. Step DCDC1 from 3.3 V to 3.0 V (`I2CWR! 0x34 0x26 0x5C`, back with `0x68`) and read both again. G26's high level tracking the 3V3 cell at both settings puts pin 19 on VESP_3V3; a level that stays put means another rail |
| P2 | T24: where the BM8563's INT goes | (a) Running: arm a 10 s countdown with interrupt (`I2CWR! 0x51 0x0E 0x02`, `I2CWR! 0x51 0x0F 10`, `I2CWR! 0x51 0x01 0x01`, `I2CWR! 0x51 0x0E 0x82`); after it, `I2CRD 0x51 0x01` shows TF set, `GPIO? 35 IN` gives GPIO35's level, and `I2CRD 0x34 0x46` (the PEK IRQ latch Kleidos reads) shows whether the power key saw a press. A PEK latch with GPIO35 high is the print's wiring; GPIO35 low with no AXP192 IRQ pending is the firmware's. (b) Off: with USB's VBUS cut at the hub, arm a 30 s countdown as above, then `PMICOFFTEST 60 KEY`; a cold boot after about 30 s (the panel lights; `SYS?` after VBUS returns) shows the countdown powering the board on through PWRON. If `I2CRD 0x51 0x0E` shows the countdown cleared by the power-off path before the board goes off, arm it from a probe image instead |
| P3 | T07, T08, T09: the Hat-Bus header | Board off, USB out, multimeter: header GND to Grove G and header 5V_OUT to Grove V read 0 Ω. Powered on battery: the 3V3 cell reads VESP_3V3; the BAT cell reads the cell (compare `PMIC?`'s battery voltage), and with USB in it still reads the cell (about 4 V), not IPSOUT or VBUS (about 5 V). 5V_IN: USB cut at the hub, 5.0 V from a current-limited supply (100 mA) on the 5V_IN cell; `I2CRD 0x34 0x00` shows ACIN present (bit 7) and VBUS absent (bit 5). G36/G25: `GPIO? 25 IN` and `GPIO? 36 IN`, then tie the shared cell to GND and to 3V3 through 1 kΩ in turn: both levels following puts PAD10 and PAD4 on one header pin. G26 and G0 the same way (G0 only after boot, never held low at reset) |
| P4 | T06: the buzzer on GPIO2 | A probe image (the debug env with an LEDC tone on GPIO2, 2.7 kHz for 1 s, nothing else changed): a tone heard, and the battery discharge current rising while it plays, shows GPIO2 drives a sounder. Its circuit stays unknown |
| P5 | T21, T22, the absent IMU (re-checked on the firmware of 2026-11-17) | `I2CSCAN` on the internal bus: answers at 0x34 and 0x51 only, nothing at 0x68 or 0x69 (repeats the 2026-09-04 scan) |
| P6 | T27 (optional, after the datasheet) | Needs a GPIO drive as in P1. USB in, battery fitted: drive GPIO27 high and read `I2CRD 0x34 0x00` (VBUS usable, bit 4) and the charge state; drive it low again. VBUS dropping out of use while GPIO27 is high confirms N_VBUSEN's polarity |
