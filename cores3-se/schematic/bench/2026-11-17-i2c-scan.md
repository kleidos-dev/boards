# CoreS3 SE bench record: internal I2C bus scan and AW9523B state (2026-11-17)

Probe P3 of `../improvements.md`. One CoreS3 SE (the owner's unit), over its USB-C port on the bench hub, case closed, nothing written to any chip. Port and MAC are left out of this record.

## Setup

- Firmware: the Kleidos `cores3_se_debug` image built from the firmware of 2026-11-17 (ELF SHA-256 `21e6a668298d8db0...`, ESP-IDF 6.0.3), already on the board and left on it. The board sat on the PIN screen; no PIN was entered.
- Commands on the debug serial console: `I2CSCAN`, `REGS?`, `REGS? aw9523` (read-only register dump through the regmap engine).

## What it proves

- **A1 (BM8563 at 0x51) and A2 (FT6336U at 0x38):** both answer on the internal bus, SDA GPIO12 / SCL GPIO11, as printed; nothing answers with SDA and SCL swapped. The firmware registers the chip at each address (`REGS?`). A2's chip identity stays the 2026-11-11 register read (FOCALTECH_ID = 0x20) cited in the triage.
- **The whole `i2c` table:** 0x34 (AXP2101), 0x38 (FT6336U), 0x40 (ES7210), 0x51 (BM8563) and 0x58 (AW9523B, ID register 0x23) answer. **0x36 (AW88298) does not answer** at this moment, and the expander shows why: `OUTPUT_P0` bit 2 (`audio_amp_reset`, P0_2 on net AW_RST) is 0, so the amplifier is held in its active-low reset between sounds. That is consistent with B4 (P0_2 is the AW88298's RSTN, the amplifier's supply being AXP_PS). 0x36 itself stays a printed address (B5); this scan does not observe it.
- **Runtime state of P0 (context for C6, not a power-on reading):** the firmware has already switched P0 to push-pull (GCR 0x10) and drives `bus_out_en` (P0_1), `usb_otg_en` (P0_5) and `boost_en` (P1_7) low on the PIN screen. The expander's power-on state before the firmware runs is not observable this way (probe P4).
