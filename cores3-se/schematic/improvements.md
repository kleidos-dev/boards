# CoreS3-SE schematic: improvement triage (2026-11-17)

Working triage under the owner's rule of 2026-11-17 (AGENTS.md "Schematics"): a detail the vendor's print does not show goes in when a primary source makes it certain, a drawing error of the print is corrected when the evidence settles it, and what stays uncertain stays out and is listed as open. Applied on 2026-11-17 as the `kleidos` entries K1-K19 of `netlist.yaml`: A1 K1, A2 K2, A8 K3, B1 K4, B2 K5, B3 K6, C2 K7, C1 K8, C3 K9, C4 K10 (GPIO0) and K11 (GPIO3), C5 K12, C7 K13, C8 K14, C9 K15-K18 (U14, U17, U18, U19), C10 K19 (sourced from TI's LMV331 datasheet SLCS136V, now `lmv331-ds` in `board.json`). J1 is applied in `board.json`. On review C6, J3, F4 and J2 were settled from the datasheets and recorded in `board.json` (no drawing entry). Not applied: C11 and A11 (no entry proposed) and the open rows, which `README.md` lists under "Open questions". A "proposed K" line below names the entry's element, kind and source type as triaged.

Sources are cited by the ids of `../board.json` `sources` (`sch`, `m5-docs-2024`, `axp2101-ds`, `esp32s3-ds`, `bm8563-ds`, `aw9523b-ds`, `sy7088-ds`, `me1502-ds`, `ili9342c-ds`) and by file path and symbol in the Kleidos firmware repository. Status values: **settled** (certain now, ready to become an entry), **settled, optional** (certain, but of low value for the drawing), **settled, no change** (the drawing already agrees), **needs bench** (a non-invasive probe below settles it), **needs source** (a primary document must be fetched and cited first), **open** (cannot be settled without opening the board; stays out and stays listed in README.md).

## Bench probes (non-invasive)

Each runs on the owner's CoreS3 SE over its USB-C port, with nothing opened and nothing written to eFuse; none is needed for the "settled" items above, they confirm them or close the "needs bench" ones.

| Probe | What it settles | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | C1, C2: VDD_SPI is not forced by eFuse, and the strap value the ROM latched. | `espefuse.py --port <port> summary` (read-only; never `burn_*`): VDD_SPI_FORCE and VDD_SPI_TIEH both 0. Also record the ROM banner's `boot:0x..` strapping word from the serial log at a cold boot. |
| P2 | B3 and which of the two printed flash alternatives U6 is on this unit. | `esptool.py --port <port> flash_id` (read-only): manufacturer 0xC8 GigaDevice or 0xEF Winbond, device 0x4018 (128 Mbit). The `cores3_se_debug` boot log's `esp_psram` lines give the PSRAM size and mode (8 MB, quad). |
| P3 | A1, A2 and the whole `i2c` table in one record. | `cores3_se_debug` console: `I2CSCAN` on the internal bus; expect exactly 0x34, 0x36, 0x38, 0x40, 0x51, 0x58. Save the transcript as the bench record the entries cite. |
| P4 | C6's consequence: what the power path does between power-on and the firmware's AW9523B init. | Hold the reset key until LED2 lights (ROM download mode: AXP_PG low has reset the expander, and no firmware runs), then measure Grove port A pin 3 (BUS_OUT) to pin 4 (GND) with a meter, with nothing on the M5-Bus or the Grove port, powered from USB. 0 V means the boost or U14 is off at the expander's power-on defaults. |
