# B1: the main I2C bus, with the L3B rail on and off (2026-11-17)

Probe B1 of `../improvements.md`. An open StickS3 on the bench hub's USB, case closed, nothing opened or probed inside. No `PERIPH` name switches L3B on this board (`PERIPH?` lists none whose mechanism is PYG2), so the rail was switched through the M5PM1's GPIO output register, the same PYG2 write the firmware makes on every boot and before every deep sleep (`src/hal/common/power_manager_m5_pm1.cpp` (`PowerManagerM5PM1::init`, and `setLcdPowerRail( false )` on the way to sleep)), and the register was put back to its read value straight after.

Firmware: Kleidos `sticks3_debug`, built from the firmware of 2026-11-17 (ELF SHA-256 prefix `13cf0d745130259a`), flashed for this probe. The same scan was also run before the flash on the image then on the board (a `sticks3_debug` image, ELF prefix `55ad934550a7662f`) with the same answer.

## Commands

1. `I2C?` with L3B up (the PIN screen; the scan tries 0x08 to 0x77 on GPIO47 SDA, GPIO48 SCL, and on the swapped pair).
2. `PERIPH OFF PANEL` (the panel to SLPIN first, as the firmware does before it drops L3B), `REGR m5pm1 GPIO_OUTPUT` (read `0x06`: PYG1 and PYG2 high), `REGW! m5pm1 GPIO_OUTPUT 0x02` (PYG2 low), `REGR m5pm1 GPIO_INPUT`, `I2C?` twice.
3. `REGW! m5pm1 GPIO_OUTPUT 0x06` (back to the read value), `REGR m5pm1 GPIO_INPUT`, `I2C?`.
4. A reset through the USB-JTAG RTS line (no download mode), then `VER?`, `HALDIAG`, `PMIC?`, `FB?`: the board came back on the PIN screen with the PMIC read normally.

## Results

| Step | PYG2 (GPIO_INPUT) | `I2C?` on SDA 47 / SCL 48 | Idle levels | Swapped pins |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L3B on | 1 | `0x18, 0x68, 0x6E` | SDA 1, SCL 1 | none |
| L3B off | 0 | `0x68, 0x6E` (twice) | SDA 1, SCL 1 | — |
| L3B on again | 1 | `0x18, 0x68, 0x6E` | SDA 1, SCL 1 | — |

## What it proves

- The bus carries three devices and only three, at 0x18, 0x68 and 0x6E: the bench agrees with F1 (M5PM1 at 0x6E), F2 (BMI270 at 0x68) and F3 (ES8311 at 0x18).
- 0x18 leaves the bus when PYG2 drops L3B and returns when it is raised, so the device at 0x18 is powered from L3B, as the print has the ES8311 (U18) on that rail.
- With L3B off the bus still idles high and the other two devices still answer: the unpowered codec side does not hold SDA or SCL low, which is what the Q4/Q5 isolation on the print is for.
- F10: nothing on the LCD flex answers on J6's I2C pins at any address from 0x08 to 0x77 while L3B (the panel's rail) is up. This shows that no I2C device answers there; it does not show what the flex connects those pins to.
