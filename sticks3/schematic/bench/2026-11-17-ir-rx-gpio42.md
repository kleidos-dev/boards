# B4: GPIO42 (IR receiver line) with GROVE_5V off and on (2026-11-17)

Probe B4 of `../improvements.md`, the half that needs no remote control: what GPIO42 (net `G42_IR_RX`) reads with the 5 V output off and on. An open StickS3 on the bench hub's USB, case closed. No IR remote was at the bench, so the "key held at the IR window" step was not run. GROVE_5V (the receiver's supply, U101 pin 2) was read with the M5PM1's own ADC on its EXT_5V_ADC pin (U16 pin 19, R21/R22 10K/10K from GROVE_5V), register `M5PM1_REG_5VINOUT_L/H` 0x26/0x27 in mV (m5stack/M5PM1, `src/M5PM1.h:229-230`, commit `315d2899c`).

Firmware: Kleidos `sticks3_debug`, built from the firmware of 2026-11-17 (ELF SHA-256 prefix `13cf0d745130259a`). The same readings were taken first on the image found on the board (ELF prefix `55ad934550a7662f`) with the same answer.

## Commands

`GPIO? 42 IN` (live pad state with the input buffer forced on), `I2CRD 0x6E 0x22 6`, `PERIPH! ON EXT5V` (POWER_CONFIG bit 3, readback `0x0F`), `GPIO? 42 IN` 150 times at about 8 reads per second, `PERIPH! OFF EXT5V` (readback `0x07`).

## Results

| State | GROVE_5V (M5PM1 ADC) | GPIO42 pad | GPIO42 level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EXT5V off | 926 to 970 mV | input, no internal pull-up or pull-down (`pu=0 pd=0`) | 0 in 60 of 60 reads |
| EXT5V on | 5,102 to 5,108 mV | same | 0 in 150 of 150 reads (and 5 of 5 on the earlier image) |

## What it proves

- With the receiver's supply up (GROVE_5V at 5.1 V) the ESP32-S3 reads GPIO42 low at idle. That agrees with D6's prediction (the VSOP383's internal pull-up against R106 10K and R105 20K leaves about 1.6 V at the pin, below VIH) and with the print's R105/R106 divider.
- It does not settle A10. A fitted IR daughterboard (receiver idle at about 1.6 V by D6) reads 0 on a digital pin, and so would a line with no receiver on it at all; GPIO42 has no ADC channel to tell them apart. A10 stays open: it needs the voltage on the line (a meter on HAT2 pin 17, outside the case) or a remote's burst seen as a change.
- Side observation, not used in any claim: GROVE_5V sits at about 0.93 V with the 5 V output off and USB in, so the rail is not at 0 V when EXT5V is off.
