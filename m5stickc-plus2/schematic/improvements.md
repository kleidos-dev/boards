# M5StickC Plus2 schematic: candidate additions and corrections (triage)

Triage of every place where the drawing could improve on the vendor's print v0.5, under the owner's rule of 2026-11-17 (AGENTS.md, "Schematics"): a detail goes in when a primary source makes it certain, a drawing error of the print is corrected when the evidence settles it, and what stays uncertain stays out and is listed as open. Each settled item names the `kleidos` entry it would become (`element` as in docs/models-and-drawings.md, "Kleidos additions and corrections").

Applied on 2026-11-17 as `kleidos` K1-K15 in `netlist.yaml` (README.md, "Kleidos additions and corrections"): I1 → K1; I2 → K2; I3 → K3; I4 → K4, K5, K6; I5 → K7; I6 → K8-K11; I8 → K12 (GPIO12 only, as the datasheet's requirement that MTDI be low at reset; what holds it low on this board stays open); I9 → K13; I13 → K14; I19 → K15. A fact about a pin the print already connects is marked on its net's labels (`{net: ...}`) rather than on the pin, because a `pin` addition draws the pin's wire dotted, as an added connection, and these connections are printed. I11 now cites the LRC datasheet in `netlist.yaml` and README.md; I23 and B-1 to B-6 are applied to `board.json`. Not applied: I10, which a review of 2026-11-17 found to confirm the print (the GS321 datasheet's Figure 1 numbers the GS321 1 IN+, 2 VSS, 3 IN-, 4 OUT, 5 VDD, as the print does; the 1 OUT, 3 IN+, 4 IN- pinout read first is the GS321Y's), I7 (low value), and every item marked open; README.md, "Open questions", lists them.

Status words: **settled** (a primary source makes it certain; the entry is ready to write), **owner** (settled, but whether it earns a marker is the owner's call), **confirms** (the evidence agrees with the print; no entry), **bench** (a non-invasive probe listed below settles it), **open** (cannot be settled without opening the board, or the source is unreadable; stays out). Kleidos firmware citations are of the firmware repository.

## Bench probes (non-invasive: USB and the STICKIO header only)

Run on 2026-11-17 with the board on USB and its battery fitted, case closed, on Kleidos `m5stickc_plus2_debug` (the image found on the board, ELF SHA-256 prefix `b1635f8975dd32f8`, built from the Kleidos firmware of 2026-11-17). The board was left on that image.

- **B1** I2C scan, `I2C?`. Done, three runs after fresh resets: only `0x51` and `0x68` answer on SDA GPIO21 / SCL GPIO22, on both controllers; nothing answers with the pins swapped; both lines idle high with the chip's pulls off. Settles I20, corroborates I1, and confirms that the bus has external pull-ups (R13, R10; their value is not measured). Record: `bench/2026-11-17-i2c-scan.md`.
- **B2** Panel read-back on the data line, `SPIRD`. Done, three runs: RDID1-3 return `0x85 0x85 0x52`, RDDPM `0x9C` (display on, sleep out), RDDMADCTL `0x08` (rotation 2, BGR, as the firmware set it), RDDCOLMOD agrees with 16-bit, every byte shifted one bit right (`SPIRD 0x04 3` prints `0x42 0xc2 0xc2`, not the documented `0x85 0x85 0x52`). The panel drives pin 4 of the LCD connector: settles I19 and B-4. Record: `bench/2026-11-17-panel-readback-spird.md`.
- **B3** Rail values at the header (multimeter between STICKIO pin 1 and pins 2, 6, 7, 8, on battery and on USB). Open: not run. It needs a person with a multimeter at the header; no instrument on this bench is wired to the Plus2 (the INA228 rig is on the T-Deck's battery lead), and the firmware cannot read its own rails. I17 stays open; I22 stays confirmed by the netlist's reading only.
- **B4** Switched off on battery, pins 2, 6, 7 read again. Open: not run, for the same reason; a switched-off board reports nothing over USB. I21 and I24 stay confirmed by the print only.
- **B5** Long hold of the power key on battery, header pin 7 on the meter. Open: not run; it needs a hand on the power key and a meter.
- **B6** (optional) charge current on a USB power meter. Open: not run; it needs the meter and a person.

Cannot be settled without opening the case (stay open): the fitted part behind U4 (I12) and U8's marking (I10), the cell's switched-off current at its leads (I24's figure), the undrawn MPU-6886 pads (I14).

## board.json against the evidence

- **B-1** `usb-uart.specs.supply` and `unused_pins` use the print's CP2104 names (REGIN, VDD, VPP, GPIO.0-GPIO.3, NC); the CH9102F's are VDD5 (7), V3 (6), GPIO4 (16), TXS/GPIO0, RXS/GPIO1, TNOW/GPIO2, WAKEUP/GPIO3 (14-11), ACT# (10). It should also say `+VDD` is the chip's own 3.3 V V3 output (I2, I3). Applied to `board.json` on 2026-11-17: the chip's own names, with the print's beside them.
- **B-2** `power-off-timer.specs`: "non_inverting_input: pin 1", "inverting_input: pin 3", "output: pin 4" are the print's numbers, and the GS321 datasheet's Figure 1 agrees with them (I10, corrected on review: the datasheet's GS321Y puts OUT on 1, IN+ on 3, IN- on 4). Applied as a `pin_numbering` note that cites it.
- **B-3** `esd-3v3.notes`: "The designators of the two USB data-line diodes ... are not legible"; the vendor PDF's bookmarks name them D17 and D18 (I23). Applied to `board.json` on 2026-11-17.
- **B-4** `display-controller.specs.interface`: "SPI, write only on this board (no MISO line on the connector)"; the firmware runs it 3-wire with reads on the SDA line, and B2 read the panel's registers back on it (I19, `bench/2026-11-17-panel-readback-spird.md`). The interface is 3-wire SPI with a bidirectional SDA. Applied to `board.json` on 2026-11-17.
- **B-5** `power-switch-fet.specs.type`: "MOSFET, drawn as P-channel"; the LRC datasheet makes it a P-channel MOSFET in DFN2020-6S outright (I11). Applied to `board.json` on 2026-11-17, with the pins.
- **B-6** `soc.specs.unused_pins` lists IO20, SD2/IO9, SD3/IO10, CMD/IO11, CLK/IO6, SD0/IO7, SD1/IO8 as "left unconnected on the board": true outside the package, but 28-31 are the in-package flash and PSRAM lines (I6); `supply` should say VDD_SDIO is an output at 3.3 V (I5). Applied to `board.json` on 2026-11-17.
- **B-7** `backlight-switch` has no word on EN's internal pull-down; add it once I12 is settled for both printed parts. Still open: I12 is settled for the SGM2578 only.
