# B1: the I2C bus answers at 0x51 and 0x68 only (2026-11-17)

Probe B1 of `../improvements.md` (items I1 and I20). The case was not opened; the board was on USB with its battery fitted.

Firmware: Kleidos `m5stickc_plus2_debug`, the image found on the board (ELF SHA-256 prefix `b1635f8975dd32f8`, built from the Kleidos firmware of 2026-11-17). The chip was identified first as an ESP32-PICO-V3-02 (revision v3.0), and its MAC matched the bench inventory's entry for this board.

## Command

`I2C?` (`I2CSCAN`): the console reads both lines with no pull and then with the chip's pull-up, then probes every 7-bit address from 0x08 to 0x77 at 100 kHz on the board's bus (SDA GPIO21, SCL GPIO22), on both I2C controllers, and on the two pins swapped as a control. The scan was run three times, each after a fresh reset, with the same result.

## Result

| Bus | Pins | Idle, no pull (SDA, SCL) | Addresses that answered |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| port 1 | SDA 21, SCL 22 | 1, 1 | `0x51`, `0x68` |
| port 0 | SDA 21, SCL 22 | 1, 1 | `0x51`, `0x68` |
| port 1, swapped | SDA 22, SCL 21 | 1, 1 | none |
| port 0, swapped | SDA 22, SCL 21 | 1, 1 | none |

The boot log of the same image names both answers: `Bm8563: ready at 0x51` and `Imu: MPU6886-compatible IMU detected at 0x68` (`MPU6886: ... ready (id=0x19 ...)`).

## What it proves

- The BM8563 RTC (U9) answers at 0x51, as its datasheet gives (I1, corroborated on the board).
- The MPU-6886 (U10) answers at 0x68, and nothing else is on the bus with the STICKIO and Grove connectors empty (I20 settled).
- Nothing answers with SDA and SCL swapped, so SDA is GPIO21 and SCL is GPIO22, as drawn.
- Both lines read high with the chip's own pulls off, so the bus has external pull-ups (R13 and R10 on the print). The probe does not measure their value.
