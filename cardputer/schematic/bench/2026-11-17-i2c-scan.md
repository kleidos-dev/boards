# Cardputer-Adv: internal I2C scan and chip identities (P01)

- Date: 2026-11-17.
- Unit: the bench Cardputer-Adv, case closed, on USB from the bench hub, power switch on.
- Firmware: the Kleidos `cardputer_debug` image already on the board, left by another bench job and not reflashed: built from the Kleidos firmware of 2026-11-17 with one uncommitted change in `src/states/settings/settings_actions.cpp`.
- Tool: the debug serial console over the board's USB Serial/JTAG port, opened without touching DTR/RTS (no reset).
- Settles: T14 (dated bench record for the ES8311 at 0x18), T18 (confirmation).

## What was run and what came back

`I2CALL?` (every candidate bus the console knows; only the Cardputer's own line is quoted, port 1):

```text
I2C bus=cardputer_adv port=1 sda=8 scl=9 idle_sda=1 idle_scl=1 pull_sda=1 pull_scl=1 ready=1 addrs=0x18,0x34,0x69
I2C bus=cardputer_adv_rev port=1 sda=9 scl=8 ... addrs=none
```

`I2C?` (the variant's bus, both controllers):

```text
I2C bus=variant port=1 sda=8 scl=9 idle_sda=1 idle_scl=1 pull_sda=1 pull_scl=1 ready=1 addrs=0x18,0x34,0x69
I2C bus=variant_p0 port=0 sda=8 scl=9 idle_sda=1 idle_scl=1 pull_sda=1 pull_scl=1 ready=1 addrs=0x18,0x34,0x69
```

Single-register reads (`I2CRD <addr> <reg> 1`):

| Address | Register | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0x18 | 0xFD | 0x83 | ES8311 chip ID 1 |
| 0x18 | 0xFE | 0x11 | ES8311 chip ID 2 |
| 0x18 | 0xFF | 0x01 | ES8311 version register |
| 0x19 | 0xFD | NACK | no device (the ES8311 with CE high would answer here) |
| 0x68 | 0x00 | NACK | no device (the BMI270 with SDO low would answer here) |
| 0x69 | 0x00 | 0x24 | BMI270 CHIP_ID |
| 0x34 | 0x01 | 0x03 | TCA8418 CFG (answers) |

A burst `I2CRD 0x18 0xFD 2` returned `0x83 0xff`: the ES8311 does not auto-increment on a read, so each register was read on its own.

## Also read in the same session (TCA8418 GPIO registers, bears on T33)

`REGR tca8418 <reg>`: `KP_GPIO1` 0x7F, `KP_GPIO2` 0xFF, `KP_GPIO3` 0x00 (ROW7, COL8, COL9 are not in the key matrix); `GPIO_DIR1-3` 0x00 (all inputs); `GPIO_PULL1-3` 0x00 (0 = pull-up enabled, so every internal pull-up is on); `GPIO_DAT_STAT1` 0xFF, `GPIO_DAT_STAT2` 0xFF, `GPIO_DAT_STAT3` 0x03 (ROW7, COL8 and COL9 read high). The unconnected ROW7, COL8 and COL9 sit high on the chip's own pull-ups, as T33 says; this does not by itself rule out a resistor the print does not show.

## Result

The internal bus on G8 (SDA) / G9 (SCL) carries exactly three devices: 0x18 (ES8311, chip ID 0x83 0x11), 0x34 (TCA8418) and 0x69 (BMI270, chip ID 0x24). 0x19 and 0x68 do not answer, which fits CE on AGND for the ES8311 and SDO high for the BMI270. The ES8311 ID matches the value in Espressif's es8311 component; the ES8311 datasheet read for T14 gives the address, not the ID register.

## Side effects noted

- `I2CALL?` leaves each pad it tried as an I2C pad with the internal pull-up on (G0, G1, G2, G11, G45 among them). The GPIO readings of P02 were therefore taken after a reset, never after this scan.
- One candidate bus of the scan (SDA on G10) reported every address as answering: G10 sits at half the cell voltage through the 100 kΩ divider (T21), which reads as a low SDA, so every address looks acknowledged. No device is on G10.
