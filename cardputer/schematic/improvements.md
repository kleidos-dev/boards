# Cardputer-Adv schematic: triage of candidate improvements

Working triage under the owner's rule of 2026-11-17 (AGENTS.md, "Schematics"): a detail the vendor's print does not show goes in when a primary source makes it certain, a drawing error of the print is corrected when the evidence settles it, and each is a `kleidos` entry in `netlist.yaml`, marked on the drawing and listed on page 1. The settled items are applied as `kleidos` entries K1-K19 in `netlist.yaml` (2026-11-17): T04 as K1-K3, T26 K4, T28 K5, T29 K6, T33 K7, T25 K8, T14 K9, T02 K10-K11, T15 K12 (correction), T32 K13, K14, K16, K17, T20 K15, T11 K18, T07 K19 (correction). A fact about a part the print already draws is marked on the net, rail, pin or table row that carries it, not on the part, because the generator draws a part addition's wires dotted, as an added connection. What is still open is listed in README.md, "Open questions". Firmware citations are the Kleidos firmware repository (2026-11-17). Datasheets read on 2026-11-17: ESP32-S3 Series Datasheet v2.2 (Espressif); ES8311 datasheet Revision 7.0 (Everest, January 2020, copy at <https://dl.espressif.com/dl/schematics/Audio_ES8311.pdf>); TCA8418 SCPS215G (TI, revised June 2018); LP3218DT1G Rev.B May 2017 (Leshan Radio, copy at <https://static.chipdip.ru/lib/495/DOC013495055.pdf>); CN803/809/810 Rev 0 (Consonance, copy at <https://static.chipdip.ru/lib/180/DOC014180357.pdf>); NS4150 user manual V1.0 (Nsiway, May 2011, hosted by Espressif as <https://dl.espressif.com/dl/schematics/NS4150B.pdf>); TP4057 (NanJing Top Power, copy at <https://atta.szlcsc.com/upload/public/pdf/source/20140228/1457706997013.pdf>); AN_SY8089/SY8089A Rev 0.9A (Silergy, copy at <https://static.chipdip.ru/lib/930/DOC012930027.pdf>); the AW35122FDR product page (<https://www.awinic.com/en/productDetail/AW35122FDR>).

Status words: **settled** (a primary source makes it certain now; the element it would take is given), **needs bench** (a non-invasive probe, listed at the end, settles it), **needs datasheet** (one document to read; not read in this pass), **open** (cannot be settled without opening the board; stays out), **no entry** (the print is right, or the fix is text of ours, not an entry).

## Bench probes (all non-invasive, case closed)

Run on 2026-11-17 where the probe needs no person at the bench. The Cardputer's bench lock was taken after another bench job released it; that job's `cardputer_debug` image (the Kleidos firmware of 2026-11-17 with one uncommitted settings-screen change) was left on the board rather than reflashed, because a clean build waited more than 30 minutes for a machine-wide build slot and was stopped.
