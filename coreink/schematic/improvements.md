# CoreInk schematic: triage of candidate improvements

Working triage under the owner's rule of 2026-11-17 (AGENTS.md, "Schematics"): a detail the vendor's print does not show goes in when a primary source makes it certain, a drawing error of the print is corrected when the evidence settles it, and each is a `kleidos` entry in `netlist.yaml`, marked on the drawing and listed on page 1. Applied on 2026-11-17 as Kleidos additions K1-K29 in `netlist.yaml`: T02 (K1-K4), T20/T21 (K5, K6, K7, K12, K13; K7 is GPIO5's strap only), T19 (K8-K11, K14, K15), T16 (K16), T14 (K17), T15 (K18) (K16 and K18 also cite ESP-IDF's Sleep Modes page: ext0 and ext1 take RTC GPIOs only), T22/T23 (K19-K24), T13 (K25), T26 (K26), and T27 for the rails only (K27, K28); T33 fixed in our text; T34 in the notes; B01-B03 in `board.json`. Settled since, by the owner looking at his unit on 2026-11-17, and applied the same day as K29: T04 (the LED on GPIO10 is green and lit when the pin is low), with B05 and P04 closed. Everything else stays open and is listed in the README's "Open questions". The datasheet page numbers were re-read for the entries: GPIO36-GPIO39 and Table 2's notes are on p. 13, GPIO34, GPIO35 and IO27 on p. 14, IO19 and pins 44-48 on p. 15. Firmware citations are the Kleidos firmware repository (2026-11-17). Datasheet citations: ESP32-PICO Series Datasheet v1.5 (Espressif), read 2026-11-17.

Status words: **settled** (a primary source makes it certain now; the element it would take is given), **needs bench** (a non-invasive probe, listed at the end, settles it), **needs datasheet** (one document to read; not read in this pass), **open** (cannot be settled without opening the board; stays out), **no entry** (the print is right, or the fix is text of ours, not an entry).

## Bench probes (all non-invasive, case closed)

Run on 2026-11-17 where the probe needs no person at the bench. The CoreInk was busy with another bench job, whose `m5core_ink_debug` image (built from the Kleidos firmware of 2026-11-17) was left on it rather than reflashed; the console commands ran under the port's `pio-job` lock, taking turns with that job. The multimeter probes need a person with a meter on the back socket: no meter is wired to this board (the bench's INA228 rig sits on the T-Deck's battery lead and is not rewired for this).
