# LILYGO T-Deck Plus: schematic improvements (triage)

Candidates for `kleidos` entries in `netlist.yaml` under the owner's rule of 2026-11-17: a detail the vendor's print leaves out goes in when a primary source makes it certain, a drawing error of the print is corrected when the evidence settles it, and what stays uncertain is listed as open. The settled items were applied on 2026-11-17 as `kleidos` entries K1 to K20 ("Applied" below); the open ones are listed in README.md, "Open questions". Firmware citations are to the Kleidos repository. "Print" is LILYGO's T-Deck mainboard sheet of 2023-06-01 (SHA-256 `c3dcd1de…`, read again on 2026-11-17 with `pdftotext`); "markers" are the `PI<part>0<pin>` and `NL<net>` markers Altium writes into it.

Status words: **addition** or **correction** (settled, becomes a `kleidos` entry of that kind), **carry** (on the print but missing from our drawing; not an entry), **no entry** (settled, and the drawing already says it), **needs datasheet** (a document settles it, not yet read), **needs bench** (a probe below settles it), **opening** (cannot be settled without opening the board; stays out, listed open), **out** (not a fact of the board's connections).
