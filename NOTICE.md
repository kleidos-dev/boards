## Vendor material: cited, never copied

The facts behind the drawings and measurements are documented from the vendors' public material: product pages, documentation, dimension drawings, schematics, datasheets, product photos and renders, and the CAD files M5Stack and LILYGO publish. None of those files is in this repository. They are the property of their owners and are cited by address (in each `board.json`, `sources.md` and `schematic/netlist.yaml`, and in each `model/spec.json`), with a digest where a build reads one. What is here is our own work: facts (dimensions, part numbers, pin assignments) and our own vector drawings of the products. In particular this repository holds no vendor photo or render, no reference overlay of one, no silhouette traced from a community CAD file, no STL or STEP file, no vendor schematic or datasheet, and no vendor's demo screen artwork. `_shared/check_public_tree.py` fails a tree that holds any of them.

## Schematics

`<board>/schematic/` holds a KiCad schematic of a board's connectivity, documented from the vendor's public documentation: `netlist.yaml` lists every component, pin and net and cites the documents the facts come from, by address and SHA-256, and the drawing presents them with this project's own layout and only this project's own symbols: each board's for its integrated circuits and connectors (`<board>.kicad_sym`), and the generic ones for the passive parts, the discrete semiconductors, the switches, the plain connectors and the grounds (`kleidos.kicad_sym`, drawn by `_shared/kicad_schematic.py`). Where a datasheet, the vendor's other documents, the Kleidos firmware's behavior on the board or a bench probe makes certain a detail the vendor's print leaves out or gets wrong, the drawing adds or corrects it: each is an entry of `kleidos` in `netlist.yaml`, marked on the drawing and listed with its source on page 1, and the bench records behind them are in `schematic/bench/`. It is licensed under the MIT License like the other drawings. No vendor document is in this repository: what is here are the facts of what connects where, with the values the vendor documents. Its PDF and SVGs are drawn with the Kleidos KiCad kit (`_shared/kicad/`), byte copies of the `brand` repository's `kicad/` at the commit `_shared/kicad/KIT.json` records: the color themes, the resolved style and the helper are MIT; the two drawing sheets embed the Kleidos mark and are brand's artwork under the GNU GPL version 3 or later (`LICENSES/GPL-3.0-or-later.txt`) with brand's additional terms. A schematic PDF shows that mark in its title block placed unchanged under brand's press permission (<https://github.com/kleidos-dev/brand/blob/main/TRADEMARKS.md>), which does not make the PDF a covered work: the PDF stays under the MIT License, and its metadata credits the mark. The Kleidos name and logo are subject to the terms in that TRADEMARKS.md.

No symbol of KiCad's libraries is in these files. The KiCad Libraries Team licenses its symbols under CC BY-SA 4.0 with an exception (<https://www.kicad.org/libraries/license/>) that waives the license's article 3 (attribution and share-alike) for designs that use them and the files generated from them, so such a design need not be CC BY-SA. The exception frees the design, not the symbols: a schematic file embeds full copies of each symbol's definition, which remain the KiCad Libraries Team's work, and the page leaves open whether copies of library parts inside a file count as redistributing "parts thereof", which the exception excludes. A file holding them could not honestly be offered as this project's work under the MIT License alone. So the schematics draw every symbol themselves, and the whole folder is this project's work under the MIT License.

## Board models

The 3D models (`<board>/model/<board>.glb`, the master, and `<board>/model/<board>-web.glb`, its web level of detail) are built by `_shared/build_board_glb.py` from vendor CAD files the build downloads and checks by digest; the CAD files are never copied here. Each model's `asset.copyright` and its `model/spec.json` say which of its parts come from which file and which are this project's. The master is also written as Wavefront OBJ (`<board>/model/<board>.obj`) by `_shared/export_obj.py`, from the GLB alone: the same geometry, so the same notice, which its header repeats; its material library (`<board>.mtl`) holds only this project's materials.

- **M5Stack cases** (StickS3, StickC Plus 1.1 / Plus2 / Plus SE, CoreInk, CoreS3 SE and its M5GO Battery Bottom3 base, Core2 v1.3, Cardputer ADV): re-meshed geometry from `m5stack/M5_Hardware`, licensed under the MIT License, "Copyright (c) 2021 M5Stack". A model built from that geometry carries M5Stack's MIT notice with it (`LICENSES/MIT.txt` holds the license text with both copyright lines): such a model is licensed under the MIT License, with both copyrights, M5Stack's for their case and this project's for the rest (`REUSE.toml`).
- **LILYGO T-Deck Plus**: its model is this project's own, built from no vendor file: `_shared/tdeck_plus_case.py` writes its case from the front drawing and the measurements in this repository, with what they leave open measured on LILYGO's published product pictures (never traced and never copied here). No LILYGO geometry is in it.

`models-contact-sheet.png` is a picture of the nine models, drawn from them by `_shared/models_contact_sheet.py`, and the one raster image in the tree. It shows the M5Stack case geometry as the models hold it, so it carries M5Stack's MIT notice for the eight M5Stack cases (`REUSE.toml`).

The checks' own test board, `_shared/fixtures/faces/`, is two boxes `_shared/face_fixture.py` writes as binary STL files, its drawings and the model built from them: test data of this project's code, MIT, and the only STL files in the tree (`_shared/check_public_tree.py` admits them only while their bytes are the ones that script writes).

## Inter

`fonts/inter/` holds Inter 4.1 (Regular, Medium, Bold), unmodified, from <https://github.com/rsms/inter>: "Copyright (c) 2016 The Inter Project Authors", licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`fonts/inter/OFL.txt`, `LICENSES/OFL-1.1.txt`). It sets the printed legends of the board models; the triangulated glyphs in a model are an object made from the font's outlines, not the font. Pins in `fonts/VERSIONS.md`.

## Khronos PBR Neutral tone mapper

`_shared/model_raster.py`, which draws the board models for `models-contact-sheet.png` and for the checks' views, tone maps a lit face with the function of `PBR_Neutral/pbrNeutral.glsl` in <https://github.com/KhronosGroup/ToneMapping> (commit `5c335949e`): "Copyright 2024 The Khronos Group, Inc.", licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (`LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt`). Changed from that file: the function is written in Python on arrays of colors (`pbr_neutral()`), operation for operation; that file carries no NOTICE of its own. The rest of the script is this project's, so the file is licensed MIT AND Apache-2.0 (`REUSE.toml`).
